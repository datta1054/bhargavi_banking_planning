### 🧠 Mock Test 1 Analysis Framework
After Mock 1, categorize every wrong question:
- **Type A — Concept gap**: Didn't know the formula/method
- **Type B — Careless error**: Knew it but made arithmetic/reading mistake
- **Type C — Time pressure**: Ran out of time, left unattempted
- **Type D — Guessed wrong**: Attempted without knowing — negative marking

> Focus fix: Type A (learn it), Type B (slow down 10%), Type C (improve selection strategy)
