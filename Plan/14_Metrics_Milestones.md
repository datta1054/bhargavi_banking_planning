# 14 · Monthly Success Metrics & Milestones
### Tracking Your Progress — From Beginner to Exam-Ready

---

## 14.1 Why Metrics Matter

Without measurement, preparation becomes aimless. Monthly metrics serve three purposes:
1. **Confirmation:** You're on track and making progress.
2. **Early warning:** You spot problems before they become disasters.
3. **Motivation:** Seeing numbers improve is one of the most powerful motivators.

**Rule:** Check your metrics at the END of every month. If you're below target, adjust — don't panic. If you're on target, celebrate briefly and move forward.

## 14.5 Motivational Milestones — Celebrate These!

These are moments worth acknowledging:

🎉 **First 100 questions practiced** → You've started the journey!  
🎉 **First correct puzzle set solved** → Reasoning is clicking!  
🎉 **First RC passage fully understood** → English is improving!  
🎉 **First 50%+ in a mock test** → You're exam-aware!  
🎉 **First 60%+ in a mock test** → You're becoming competitive!  
🎉 **First time all sectional cutoffs cleared** → Complete performance!  
🎉 **First 65%+ in a mock test** → You're in the clearing zone!  
🎉 **First mock test where you felt calm throughout** → Mental mastery!  
🎉 **Completed all monthly milestones** → You're following the plan!  

> **Treat preparation as a series of small wins, not one big gamble.**
