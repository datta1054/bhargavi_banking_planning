## 13.1 The Interview Phase — Why it Matters More Than You Think

For exams that have interviews (IBPS PO, IBPS RRB PO, Karnataka Bank PO), the final selection formula is:

```
IBPS PO Final Score = Mains Score (80%) + Interview Score (20%)
```

This means a candidate who scores 130/200 in Mains but only 55/100 in Interview gets:
→ (130/200 × 80) + (55/100 × 20) = 52 + 11 = **63 points**

While someone who scores 120/200 in Mains but 75/100 in Interview gets:
→ (120/200 × 80) + (75/100 × 20) = 48 + 15 = **63 points**

They're equal! **A great interview can compensate for a weaker Mains score.**

> **Start interview preparation from Month 3 (October 2026)** — not after clearing Mains.

## 13.2 Banking Interview Format

### IBPS PO Interview Panel
- **Duration:** 15–25 minutes
- **Panel Members:** 4–5 (mix of bankers and IBPS officials)
- **Marks:** 100
- **Languages:** English primarily (Hindi accepted in some regions)

### Typical Interview Structure

| Time Block | What Happens |
|-----------|-------------|
| First 3–5 min | Introduction, "Tell me about yourself," why banking |
| Next 5–8 min | Banking/financial awareness questions |
| Next 3–5 min | Subject-related questions (B.Com background) |
| Next 2–3 min | Situational/HR questions |
| Last 2 min | Your questions to the panel (optional) |

## 13.3 Interview Preparation — 5 Pillars

### Pillar 1: Self Introduction & Personal Background

The "Tell me about yourself" question is the **most important 2 minutes of your interview**. A bad start demoralizes the panel and you. A great start sets a confident tone.

**Structure for 2-minute Self-Introduction:**
```
1. Name + Educational Background (15 seconds)
2. Why banking as a career (30 seconds)
3. Any relevant experience or internship (15 seconds)
4. Key strengths relevant to banking (30 seconds)
5. Closing: Readiness and enthusiasm (15 seconds)
```

**Example (Customize to your situation):**

> "Good morning/afternoon. My name is Bhargavi, and I am from [City]. I have recently completed my Bachelor of Commerce from [College Name], which gave me a strong foundation in financial principles, accounting, and economics.
>
> I have always been fascinated by the banking sector because of its direct impact on economic development and financial inclusion. The opportunity to serve customers, manage financial transactions, and contribute to India's growth makes banking a deeply purposeful career for me.
>
> During my B.Com, I developed analytical skills and financial awareness, which I believe align well with the demands of a banking role. I am also a disciplined learner — I have been systematically preparing for banking examinations for the past several months, which reflects my commitment to this career.
>
> I am excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Bank Name] and grow as a banking professional. Thank you."

**Practice this every morning for 5 minutes — until it flows naturally.**

### Pillar 2: Banking and Financial Awareness

This is where most candidates fail interviews. You must be able to discuss:

#### Must-Know Banking Interview Topics

**RBI & Monetary Policy (Most Asked)**
| Topic | Key Points to Know |
|-------|------------------|
| Functions of RBI | Issuing currency, regulator of banks, lender of last resort, managing forex reserves, government banker |
| Repo Rate | Rate at which RBI lends to commercial banks; currently [check latest rate] |
| Reverse Repo Rate | Rate at which RBI borrows from commercial banks |
| CRR | Cash Reserve Ratio — % of deposits kept with RBI (currently 4%) |
| SLR | Statutory Liquidity Ratio — % of deposits in govt. securities (currently 18%) |
| MSF | Marginal Standing Facility — emergency borrowing by banks from RBI |
| Bank Rate | Rate at which RBI rediscounts bills of exchange |

> **Action:** Check the RBI website or Moneycontrol for the CURRENT repo rate, reverse repo rate, CRR, and SLR before your interview. These change with each policy review.

**Types of Banks**
- Scheduled Commercial Banks (PSBs, Private Sector Banks, Foreign Banks)
- Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)
- Co-operative Banks
- Small Finance Banks (Ujjivan, Jana, AU, etc.)
- Payments Banks (Paytm, Airtel, India Post)
- Development Banks (NABARD, NHB, SIDBI, EXIM Bank)

**Recent Banking Developments (Interview Favorites 2026–27)**
- Digital Rupee (CBDC) — what it is, current status
- UPI 2.0 / UPI Credit Line
- Account Aggregator Framework
- NARCL (National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited) — bad bank
- RBI's Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) framework
- Financial Literacy initiatives
- Priority Sector Lending norms
- GST and its impact on banking
- Basel III norms compliance
- Open Banking

**Non-Performing Assets (NPAs)**
- What is an NPA? (Loan not repaid for 90+ days)
- Types: Sub-standard, Doubtful, Loss Assets
- Gross NPA vs. Net NPA
- SARFAESI Act — banks can recover assets without court order
- IBC (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code) 2016 — resolution of NPAs
- Why NPAs matter: Weakens bank's capital, reduces lending ability

### Pillar 3: B.Com-Specific Questions

The interview panel WILL ask about your academic background. Be prepared with:

**Expected B.Com Questions:**
1. What is a Balance Sheet? (Assets = Liabilities + Equity)
2. What is the difference between cash accounting and accrual accounting?
3. What is Working Capital?
4. What are the functions of a commercial bank? (Accepting deposits, granting loans)
5. What is GST? How does it work?
6. What is Fiscal Deficit? Revenue Deficit?
7. How does inflation affect banking?
8. What is the difference between a stock and a bond?
9. Explain the concept of depreciation.
10. What is the difference between microeconomics and macroeconomics?

**Your advantage:** These are your B.Com subjects. You should know these confidently. Review your B.Com notes (especially Economics and Accountancy) in Month 8–9 for interview.

### Pillar 4: Situational/Behavioral Questions (HR Round)

These questions test how you would behave in real banking scenarios.

| Common Question | How to Answer |
|----------------|--------------|
| "Why do you want to join banking?" | Connect to values: stability, service, financial inclusion, career growth |
| "Why this specific bank?" | Research the bank — mention recent initiatives, its reach, CSR |
| "How do you handle an angry customer?" | Listen → Empathize → Apologize → Resolve → Follow up |
| "What would you do if you find a fraud in your branch?" | Report immediately to senior/manager, not handle alone |
| "Are you comfortable being posted in rural areas?" | Be honest but positive — emphasize adaptability |
| "What are your weaknesses?" | Mention a real weakness but show you're improving it |
| "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" | Progressing through bank ranks, taking on more responsibilities |
| "How do you handle stress?" | Organized planning, prioritization, staying calm under deadlines |

**The STAR Method for behavioral answers:**
- **S**ituation: Set the context
- **T**ask: What was required of you
- **A**ction: What you did
- **R**esult: What outcome happened

### Pillar 5: Current Affairs & General Knowledge

**What the interview panel asks about:**
- Last 3 months of major economic/banking news
- Recent government budget decisions
- New banking regulations
- Recent appointments (RBI Governor, Finance Minister, Bank CEOs)
- Economic indicators (GDP growth rate, inflation rate, etc.)

**Preparation:** 
- Keep a "Interview Current Affairs" notebook from Month 3
- Note only banking/economy/government news
- Maintain for 6 months before interview

### Mock Interview Practice System

**From April 2027 onwards:**

**Self-Mock (Mirror practice):**
- Stand in front of mirror
- Ask yourself a question
- Answer it aloud, naturally
- Evaluate: Did I maintain eye contact (with mirror)? Was my body language confident? Did I answer completely?

**Partner Mock Interview:**
- Ask a family member or friend to interview you
- Give them a list of 20 common questions
- Record the session if possible
- Review your answer quality, language, and confidence

**Recording Method (Best):**
- Record yourself on phone answering 5 questions
- Watch it: Voice modulation? Unnecessary fillers (um, er)? Clarity of thought?

## 13.5 Personality & Communication Tips

### Body Language in Interview
- **Entering:** Knock, ask permission, walk in confidently
- **Sitting:** Straight back, no slouching, hands on table (not fidgeting)
- **Eye contact:** Maintain with the questioner, briefly acknowledge others on panel
- **Smile:** Natural — not forced
- **Nodding:** When listening — shows engagement

### Language Tips
- Speak clearly and at moderate pace — not too fast (anxiety) or too slow (boring)
- If you don't know an answer: "I'm not sure of the exact figure, but to the best of my knowledge, [attempt an answer]. I'll confirm and update."
- Don't use excessive English jargon if you're not confident — clear, simple sentences are better
- End complex answers with: "Would you like me to elaborate on any aspect?"

### Dress Code
- **Women:** Formal salwar-kameez (preferred for banking) or formal saree. Conservative colors (blue, grey, white, black). Minimal jewelry.
- Hair: Tied neatly
- No excessive makeup or perfume
- Footwear: Clean formal sandals or flat shoes

## 13.6 Karnataka Bank-Specific Interview Preparation

Karnataka Bank conducts personal interviews for PO positions. Additional preparation:

**Karnataka-Specific Topics:**
- History and presence of Karnataka Bank (Founded 1924, HQ: Mangaluru)
- Karnataka Bank's major services and recent initiatives
- Karnataka's economic indicators (GSDP, major industries)
- Karnataka state government schemes
- Districts where Karnataka Bank operates extensively

**Common Karnataka Bank Interview Questions:**
1. Why Karnataka Bank specifically?
2. What do you know about Karnataka Bank's history?
3. Are you comfortable working in any Karnataka Bank branch location?
4. What is Karnataka's major industry? (IT, textiles, agriculture)
5. Which are the major banking competitors of Karnataka Bank?

## 13.7 Interview Day Logistics

### Documents to Carry (IMPORTANT)
- [ ] Admit card / Call letter (print × 2)
- [ ] Valid photo ID: Aadhar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport
- [ ] Degree certificate + marksheets (original + 2 photocopies)
- [ ] 4–6 recent passport-size photographs
- [ ] Caste certificate (if applicable)
- [ ] Any experience/internship certificates
- [ ] A small notepad (for reference while waiting)
- [ ] Documents in a neat file/folder, not loose

### Arrive Early
- Reach 30–45 minutes before your slot
- This time: Calm yourself, review your introduction once, observe other candidates (learn from them)
- Do NOT discuss difficult questions with other candidates in the waiting area — it creates anxiety
