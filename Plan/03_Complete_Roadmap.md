## 3.1 Roadmap Overview

The entire preparation is divided into **4 strategic phases**, each building on the previous one. Think of it like building a house:
- **Phase 1** = Laying the foundation (cement, bricks)
- **Phase 2** = Building the walls (structure)
- **Phase 3** = Putting the roof and interior (functionality)
- **Phase 4** = Painting and finishing (exam readiness)

## 3.2 Phase 1: Foundation & Fundamentals
**Duration:** August 2026 (Week 2) → October 2026 (Week 4) | **~11 Weeks**

### Purpose & Rationale

This phase exists because banking exams test application of concepts — not just memorization. If your fundamentals are shaky, you will struggle with every problem type you encounter later. By investing 11 weeks in fundamentals now, you save months of confusion later.

**What happens in Phase 1:**
- Build arithmetic foundation (basic math operations, fractions, percentages)
- Learn grammar rules from basics for English
- Understand core reasoning concepts (logical thinking patterns)
- Begin banking awareness from scratch
- Build study habits and consistency

### Phase 1 Weekly Structure (Sample)

| Week | QA Focus | Reasoning Focus | English Focus | GA/Banking |
|------|---------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| 1–2 | Number System, BODMAS, LCM/HCF | Basic Inequalities, Directions | Parts of Speech, Tenses | What is Banking? RBI Basics |
| 3–4 | Percentages (basics + applications) | Syllogisms (All, Some, No) | Noun, Pronoun, Verb | Types of Banks, Account Types |
| 5–6 | Ratio & Proportion, Average | Coding-Decoding (basic patterns) | Subject-Verb Agreement | Monetary Policy, Repo Rate |
| 7–8 | Profit & Loss, SI & CI | Blood Relations, Seating (basic) | Articles, Prepositions | Government Schemes (basic) |
| 9–10 | Time-Speed-Distance, Time & Work | Linear Seating (complex), Order/Ranking | Comprehension (easy texts) | Current Affairs daily reading begins |
| 11 | Review + Mini-tests on Phase 1 topics | Review + Mini-tests | Review + vocabulary | Revision of GA basics |

### Phase 1 Targets (Measurable)

- [ ] Solve basic arithmetic questions (Percentage, Ratio, Profit/Loss) with 80%+ accuracy in untimed conditions
- [ ] Understand and solve Syllogism questions (all basic types) correctly
- [ ] Know 100+ banking-related terms and concepts
- [ ] Read 2–3 short English paragraphs daily with comprehension
- [ ] Have a vocabulary notebook with 150+ words
- [ ] Complete at least 500 QA practice questions total
- [ ] Complete at least 400 Reasoning practice questions total

### Phase 1 Exit Criteria

Before moving to Phase 2, you should be able to:
✅ Solve any basic arithmetic problem without calculator in reasonable time  
✅ Identify and apply basic grammar rules (10+ rules)  
✅ Solve straightforward syllogism, direction sense, and coding-decoding problems  
✅ Explain the basic structure of RBI, types of accounts, and 5 government schemes

## 3.3 Phase 2: Concept Strengthening & Topic Mastery
**Duration:** November 2026 → January 2027 (Week 2) | **~10 Weeks**

### Purpose & Rationale

Phase 1 taught you *what* the concepts are. Phase 2 teaches you *how to apply* them in exam conditions. This phase introduces more complex question types, moves from basic to intermediate level, and begins time-based practice.

**Why this phase is critical:**
- Banking exams don't just test if you know a formula — they test if you can apply it under pressure in 1–2 minutes
- DI (Data Interpretation) needs to be mastered now — it appears in every exam and carries high marks
- Complex puzzle types (Floor puzzles, Box puzzles, Month-based) take repeated practice to master
- English needs to move from grammar rules to active reading and question-solving

**What happens in Phase 2:**
- Cover all remaining topics not covered in Phase 1
- Introduce Data Interpretation (critical for IBPS PO/Clerk Mains)
- Begin timed topic-wise practice
- Increase difficulty level of practice questions
- Start banking and current affairs seriously
- Begin writing practice for IBPS PO Mains (essay + letter)

### Phase 2 Weekly Structure (Sample)

| Week | QA Focus | Reasoning Focus | English Focus | GA/Banking |
|------|---------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| 12–13 | Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar, Line) | Floor Puzzles, Box Puzzles | Reading Comprehension (medium level) | Banking Awareness deep dive |
| 14–15 | DI (Pie Chart, Mixed), Quadratic Equations | Month/Year Puzzles, Mixed Puzzles | Cloze Test, Error Detection | RBI Monetary Policy in depth |
| 16–17 | Number Series, Approximation, Simplification | Input-Output (basic) | Para Jumbles, Sentence Improvement | Indian Economy fundamentals |
| 18–19 | Mixture & Alligation, Partnership, Boats | Input-Output (complex), Data Sufficiency | Fill in the Blanks, Double Fillers | Government Schemes (comprehensive) |
| 20–21 | Probability, Permutation & Combination | Critical Reasoning | Phrase Replacement, Column questions | Budget basics, Static GK |

### Phase 2 Targets (Measurable)

- [ ] Solve DI sets (5 questions per set) in under 8 minutes with 80%+ accuracy
- [ ] Solve complex puzzles (Floor/Box/Month) independently within 10 minutes
- [ ] Attempt a Reading Comprehension passage (500 words) in 8–10 minutes with 70%+ accuracy
- [ ] Know 300+ banking terms, policies, and scheme details
- [ ] Complete first Sectional Tests (each section individually timed)
- [ ] Score 65%+ in sectional tests for QA and Reasoning
- [ ] Have 250+ words in vocabulary notebook

## 3.4 Phase 3: Intensive Practice & Full Syllabus Integration
**Duration:** January 2027 (Week 3) → March 2027 (Week 2) | **~7 Weeks**

### Purpose & Rationale

By now, you know the concepts. Phase 3 is about **building exam stamina, speed, and decision-making skills**. The single biggest differentiator between someone who "knows" the content and someone who "clears" the exam is the ability to manage time under pressure and make smart choices about which questions to attempt.

**What happens in Phase 3:**
- Begin full-length mock tests (most important activity of this phase)
- Develop question selection strategy (which questions to attempt/skip)
- Work on increasing speed without losing accuracy
- Identify and fix weak areas from mock test analysis
- Intensive revision of GA and Current Affairs (last 6 months)
- Start interview preparation for PO candidates

### Phase 3 Weekly Structure

| Week | Primary Activity | Secondary Activity | GA/Mock |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|---------|
| 22–23 | Full Mock Test × 2 + Analysis | DI and Puzzle revision | Current Affairs revision |
| 24–25 | Full Mock Test × 2 + Analysis | Speed drills for QA | Banking Awareness revision |
| 26–27 | Full Mock Test × 2 + Analysis | English speed improvement | Static GK revision |
| 28 | Exam-specific mock tests (RRB Prelims pattern) | Weak topic focus | Full GA revision |

### Phase 3 Targets (Measurable)

- [ ] Complete minimum 12 full mock tests
- [ ] Achieve Prelims-level target score: 65+ (IBPS PO), 75+ (IBPS Clerk), 60+ out of 80 (RRB)
- [ ] Reduce time-per-question in QA from 3 min to 1.5–2 min average
- [ ] Achieve 80%+ accuracy in Reasoning under timed conditions
- [ ] Complete GA revision for last 6 months of current affairs

## 3.5 Phase 4: Final Polish, Revision & Exam Readiness
**Duration:** March 2027 (Week 3) → May 2027 (Week 2) | **~7 Weeks**

### Purpose & Rationale

This is the final sprint. No new topics. Only revision, mock tests, and refinement. The goal is to convert your preparation into peak exam performance.

**What happens in Phase 4:**
- Only mock tests, previous-year papers, and revision
- Daily GA/Current Affairs capsule review
- Exam-day strategy practice
- Interview preparation intensification (for PO candidates)
- Last 30-day revision plan execution

### Phase 4 Weekly Structure

| Week | Activity |
|------|---------|
| 29–30 | 2 mock tests/week + PYQ papers (2015–2024) for IBPS |
| 31–32 | Weak area intensive revision + 2 mock tests/week |
| 33–34 | Current Affairs final revision (last 12 months) + 2 mock tests |
| 35 | Exam-day strategy, mental conditioning, last revision |

### Phase 4 Targets (Measurable)

- [ ] Complete minimum 20 total full mock tests (from Phase 3 + 4 combined)
- [ ] Solve 5 years of IBPS PO/Clerk/RRB previous-year papers
- [ ] Achieve consistent target score in the last 5 mock tests
- [ ] Complete 360-degree current affairs revision
- [ ] Complete mock interview practice (3+ mock interviews)
