# 09 · Daily Study Allocation
### Hour-by-Hour Breakdown for Every Day of Preparation

---

## 9.1 Why a Daily Allocation Plan Matters

While weekly schedules tell you **what** to study, the daily study allocation tells you **how much** time each subject gets every single day. Without this, most students unconsciously:
- Over-spend on subjects they enjoy.
- Under-spend on subjects they dislike (usually English).
- Skip General Awareness because "it can always be done later".

This daily allocation removes study bias and ensures every subject gets the exact attention it needs every day.

---

## 9.2 Daily Hour Allocation by Phase

### Phase 1 (Aug 2026 – Oct 2026): 2.5 Hours/Day
**Focus:** Foundation phase. Quantitative Aptitude gets the most time because arithmetic fundamentals require the most initial conceptual effort. English gets a consistent daily slot to build grammar basics from the ground up.

| Subject | Daily Minutes | % of Study Time | What Happens |
|---------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| Quantitative Aptitude | 70–75 min | 47% | New concept + 15–20 practice questions |
| Reasoning Ability | 50–55 min | 34% | New concept + 10–15 practice questions |
| English Language | 25–30 min | 18% | Grammar rule + practice OR reading |
| GA/Banking Awareness | 10–15 min | 8% | Reading + noting key facts |
| **Total** | **~155 min** | **~2.5 hrs** | |

---

### Phase 2 (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027): 3–3.5 Hours/Day
**Focus:** Topic mastery. Computer Awareness is introduced. Data Interpretation practice is intensive and time-consuming. English moves from grammar rules to active reading and sentence application.

| Subject | Daily Minutes | % of Study Time | What Happens |
|---------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| Quantitative Aptitude | 65–70 min | 35% | DI sets (timed) + arithmetic topic practice |
| Reasoning Ability | 60–65 min | 33% | Puzzle solving (timed) + logical review |
| English Language | 35–40 min | 19% | RC passage + daily vocabulary + cloze/jumbles |
| GA/Banking Awareness | 20–25 min | 12% | CA reading + Banking awareness topic deep dive |
| Computer Awareness | 30–35 min | 16% | New topic OR revision (first 3 weeks of Phase 2 only) |
| **Total** | **~190–210 min** | **~3–3.5 hrs** | |

> **Note on Computer:** Computer study runs for ~3 weeks in November. After that, the 30–35 minutes previously used for Computer shifts to extra QA/Reasoning practice.

---

### Phase 3 (Feb 2027 – Mid-Mar 2027): 3 Hours/Day
**Focus:** Mock test integration. The time previously spent on learning new topics shifts to mock tests and analysis. GA gets more time as Mains preparation intensifies.

| Subject | Daily Minutes | % of Study Time | What Happens |
|---------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| Mock Test / PYQ | 60 min (mock days) | 33% | Full section or full mock |
| Mock Analysis | 60 min (mock days) | 33% | Reviewing every wrong question |
| QA Practice | 50 min (non-mock days) | 28% | Timed drills, DI sets |
| Reasoning Practice | 45 min (non-mock days) | 25% | Timed puzzle sets |
| English Practice | 30 min | 17% | Timed RC + question types |
| GA Revision | 25–30 min | 15% | Current affairs + banking review |
| **Total** | **~170–180 min** | **~3 hrs** | |

> **On mock test days (Monday & Thursday in the sample schedule):**  
> → 60 min: Mock test  
> → 60 min: Analysis  
> → 30 min: GA revision  
> **Total = 2.5 hrs**

> **On non-mock days:**  
> → 50 min: QA  
> → 45 min: Reasoning  
> → 30 min: English  
> → 30 min: GA  
> **Total = 2.5–3 hrs**

---

### Phase 4 (Mid-Mar 2027 – May 2027): 2.5–3 Hours/Day
**Focus:** Mock tests, PYQ papers, and targeted revision. GA gets the highest time allocation because current affairs requires the longest review times.

| Subject | Daily Minutes | % of Study Time | What Happens |
|---------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| Mock Test / PYQ | 60 min | 33% | Full prelims mock OR PYQ paper |
| Mock Analysis | 45–60 min | 28% | Deep review of every error |
| QA Revision | 30 min | 17% | Speed drills only — simplification, DI |
| Reasoning Revision | 30 min | 17% | Puzzle re-practice, syllogisms |
| GA / CA Revision | 30–40 min | 20% | CA capsule + banking awareness |
| English Revision | 15–20 min | 9% | RC passage + grammar sheet |
| **Total** | **~165–200 min** | **~2.5–3 hrs** | |

## 9.3 Day-Type Allocation Templates

### Template A: Regular Study Day (Phase 1–2)

```
DURATION: 2.5–3 hours

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TIME BLOCK 1 — Primary Subject (75–80 min)                 │
│                                                            │
│ ► 0:00 – 0:10  │ Quick recall of yesterday's topic (no   │
│                │ book — just think through what you did) │
│ ► 0:10 – 0:45  │ New concept study (read + examples)     │
│ ► 0:45 – 1:15  │ Practice questions (15–20 questions)    │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIME BLOCK 2 — Secondary Subject (55–65 min)               │
│                                                            │
│ ► 0:00 – 0:15  │ New concept or revision of last topic   │
│ ► 0:15 – 0:55  │ Practice (12–15 questions or 1 puzzle   │
│                │ set)                                    │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIME BLOCK 3 — English (25–35 min)                         │
│                                                            │
│ ► 0:00 – 0:15  │ Grammar rule (study 1 rule) OR          │
│                │ Read one short passage (300–400 words)  │
│ ► 0:15 – 0:30  │ Practice: 10 questions (FIB, error      │
│                │ detection, or RC questions)             │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIME BLOCK 4 — GA/Banking (10–20 min)                      │
│                                                            │
│ ► Read current affairs for today                          │
│ ► Note 3–5 facts in CA notebook                           │
│ ► OR: Read one banking topic from Phase plan              │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Template B: Mock Test Day (Phase 3–4)

```
DURATION: 2.5 hours

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ BLOCK 1 — MOCK TEST (60 min, exact exam conditions)        │
│                                                            │
│ ► No phone. No notes. No interruptions.                   │
│ ► Attempt as if it's the REAL EXAM.                       │
│ ► Note time at end of each section.                       │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SHORT BREAK (15 min)                                       │
│ ► Rest. Don't check answers yet.                          │
│ ► Drink water, move around.                               │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ BLOCK 2 — MOCK ANALYSIS (60–75 min)                        │
│                                                            │
│ ► 0:00–0:15  │ Score calculation + section-wise split    │
│ ► 0:15–0:45  │ Review every wrong answer — understand    │
│              │ the correct method                        │
│ ► 0:45–0:60  │ Categorize errors (Conceptual/Calc/Read)  │
│ ► 0:60–0:70  │ Write action plan for next 2 days         │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BLOCK 3 — GA Revision (20–30 min)                          │
│                                                            │
│ ► Review CA notes from last 3 days                       │
│ ► Quick banking awareness topic read                      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Template C: College-Heavy Day

```
DURATION: 1.5–2 hours (reduced schedule on high-workload college days)

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ BLOCK 1 — Primary Revision Only (45–60 min)                │
│ ► Revise what you studied in the last 2 days               │
│ ► Solve 15 timed questions (no new concepts)               │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BLOCK 2 — English (20–25 min)                              │
│ ► 1 Grammar rule review                                    │
│ ► 5 vocabulary words                                       │
│ ► Read 1 short passage (optional)                         │
│                                                            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ BLOCK 3 — GA (15–20 min)                                   │
│ ► Current affairs only                                    │
│ ► Read Inshorts or BankersAdda daily quiz                 │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Minimum day threshold:** Even on the busiest days, 45 minutes of GA + vocabulary reading counts as "not breaking the streak." Never go to 0.

### Template D: College Exam Week (Semester Exams)

```
DURATION: 30–45 minutes only

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Only Activity (30–45 min):                                 │
│ ► Read today's current affairs (15 min)                   │
│ ► Review 5 vocabulary words (5 min)                       │
│ ► Read 1 banking awareness topic (15 min)                 │
│                                                            │
│ PURPOSE: Maintain study habit, don't lose GA momentum.    │
│ NO QA, NO Reasoning during exam week — it's fine.         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 9.4 Subject-Wise Daily Minimums (Non-Negotiable)

These are the minimum daily activities that must happen regardless of how busy the day is:

| Subject | Non-Negotiable Daily Minimum |
|---------|----------------------------|
| Quantitative Aptitude | 10 simplification questions (takes 5 min) |
| Reasoning | 10 inequality or syllogism questions (takes 5 min) |
| English | Read 1 English paragraph from any source |
| GA/Banking | Read 5 current affairs headlines |
| Vocabulary | Learn 3 new words |

**Total minimum:** ~20–25 minutes. Even on the worst day, this keeps the habit alive.

## 9.6 Morning vs Evening Studier — Optimized Schedules

### Option A: Morning Studier (Best)
```
6:30 AM  → Wake up
7:00 AM  → Session 1: QA or Reasoning (primary) — 75 min
8:15 AM  → 10-min break (water, stretch)
8:25 AM  → Session 2: Reasoning or QA (secondary) — 55 min
9:20 AM  → Session 3: English — 30 min
9:50 AM  → GA/CA reading — 15 min
10:05 AM → Done ✅
```

### Option B: Evening Studier
```
6:00 PM  → Session 1: QA or Reasoning — 75 min
7:15 PM  → 15-min dinner/break
7:30 PM  → Session 2: Reasoning or QA — 55 min
8:25 PM  → Session 3: English — 30 min
8:55 PM  → GA/CA reading — 15 min
9:10 PM  → Done ✅
```

### Option C: Split Schedule (For College Days)
```
7:00 AM  → Session 1: QA — 60 min (before college)
6:30 PM  → Session 2: Reasoning — 55 min
7:25 PM  → Session 3: English + GA — 45 min
8:10 PM  → Done ✅
```

Review this log every Saturday during your review session.
