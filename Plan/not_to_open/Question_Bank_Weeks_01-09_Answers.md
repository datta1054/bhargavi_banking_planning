## Professional (56–60)

### 56
**Answer:**
1. **Repo rate:** Raising it increases the cost to banks of obtaining short-term funds from the central bank.
2. **CRR:** Raising it requires banks to hold more deposits as reserves, leaving fewer funds available for lending.
3. **Open market operations:** Selling government securities absorbs money from the banking system; buying them injects liquidity.
4. **Transmission path:** These actions can affect bank funding and liquidity, then credit conditions and demand, which may moderate inflationary pressure over time.
5. **Limitation:** The effects are delayed and uneven because banks’ funding mix, borrower demand, expectations, and wider economic conditions differ.
