# Questions: Weeks 01–09

**Scope:** Topics introduced in weekly plans 01–09 only.

**Distribution:** 15 Basic, 25 Intermediate, 15 Advanced, 5 Professional.

## Basic (1–15)

### 1. What is the HCF of 18 and 24?

### 2. Evaluate using the order of operations: 6 + 2 × 5.

### 3. What is 15% of 200?

### 4. A price rises by 10% and then falls by 10%. Is the final price unchanged, higher, or lower?

### 5. Divide 25 in the ratio 2:3.

### 6. Find the mean of 10, 14, and 18.

### 7. Find the simple interest on ₹1,000 at 5% per year for 2 years.

### 8. A car travels 120 km in 3 hours at a constant speed. What is its speed?

### 9. What number comes next: 4, 7, 10, 13, __?

### 10. You face north, turn 90° clockwise, and then 180° anticlockwise. Which direction do you face?

### 11. Complete the sentence: “Each of the applicants ___ submitted a form.”

### 12. Which article fits: “___ honest answer”?

### 13. Complete the sentence: “She has lived here ___ 2021.”

### 14. Change to passive voice: “The staff opened a new branch.”

### 15. Which account is commonly used for regular personal deposits and withdrawals while earning interest?

## Intermediate (16–40)

### 16. An item costs ₹800 and is sold at a profit of 15%. Find the selling price.

### 17. Find the compound amount on ₹2,000 at 10% per year for 2 years, compounded annually.

### 18. If A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 5:6, find A:C.

### 19. The average of five numbers is 18. One number, 12, is replaced by 22. What is the new average?

### 20. A can complete a task in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together for 4 days, after which A leaves. How many additional days does B need to finish?

### 21. A 150-metre train passes a signal post in 10 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?

### 22. Find the next term: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, __.

### 23. Statements: All tellers are employees. Some employees are graduates. Does it follow that some tellers are graduates?

### 24. Anil is Beena’s father, and Beena is Charu’s mother. What is Anil’s relation to Charu?

### 25. Five people P, Q, R, S, and T sit in a row facing north. Q is in the middle, P is immediately to Q’s left, R is at the right end, and S is immediately to R’s left. Where does T sit?

### 26. In a circular arrangement, everyone faces the centre. If a person turns to their own right, which way around the circle do they move?

### 27. In a code, each letter is replaced by the next letter of the alphabet. How is BANK written?

### 28. A student is 12th from the left and 17th from the right. How many students are in the row?

### 29. Choose the conjunction: “___ the route was longer, the team arrived on time.”

### 30. Correct the sentence: “Neither the manager nor the employees was informed.”

### 31. Change to indirect speech: The teacher said to the class, “Please open your books.”

### 32. Change to passive voice: “The bank processes applications every day.”

### 33. What does a SWIFT code primarily help identify in an international transfer?

### 34. Give 3 reasons banks perform KYC checks.

### 35. Give 3 practical distinctions between NEFT and RTGS. Avoid assuming fixed limits or fees.

### 36. When the RBI raises the repo rate, what are 3 likely effects on banks, borrowers, or spending, and what is 1 limitation of this transmission?

### 37. What major event occurred in Indian banking in 1969?

### 38. List 3 purposes of Priority Sector Lending requirements. Avoid relying on potentially outdated target percentages.

### 39. What does crossing a cheque generally indicate?

### 40. Give 3 functions or forms of support associated with NABARD.

## Advanced (41–55)

### 41. A shop gives successive discounts of 20% and 10%. What single discount has the same effect?

### 42. ₹10,000 is invested at 10% per year for 2 years. Find the difference between annual compound interest and simple interest.

### 43. A vehicle covers the same distance at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. What is its average speed for the whole journey?

### 44. Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours, Pipe B fills it in 8 hours, and a drain empties it in 24 hours. How long will the tank take to fill if all three are open?

### 45. Identify the wrong term: 1, 8, 27, 64, 124, 216. Explain the pattern in 2 points: identify the error and state the rule.

### 46. A person walks 6 km north, 8 km east, and then 6 km south. How far and in which direction are they from the starting point?

### 47. A code assigns A=1, B=2, …, Z=26, then writes each word as the sum of its letter values. What are the codes for BANK and CASH, and which is larger?

### 48. Statements: All roses are flowers. No flower is metal. Answer in 3 points: state the conclusion that follows, explain the set relationship, and clarify whether the existence of roses is required.

### 49. Four people A, B, C, and D live on floors 1–4, where 1 is the bottom. D lives on the top floor, C lives immediately below D, B lives on the bottom floor, and A lives above B. Find each person’s floor and show the order from bottom to top.

### 50. Four boxes W, X, Y, and Z are stacked from bottom to top in positions 1–4. Z is at the top, X is immediately below Z, and W is at the bottom. Where is Y?

### 51. Arrange these sentences into a coherent sequence: (P) The bank reviewed the application. (Q) The customer submitted the required documents. (R) It then informed the customer of its decision. (S) The process began when the customer applied for a loan.

### 52. Correct both errors: “The committee have reached their decision after a lengthy deliberations.” Give the corrected sentence and identify both errors.

### 53. The RBI buys government securities from banks through an open market operation. Explain the likely immediate liquidity effect in 3 points: state the direction, explain the transaction, and give one qualification.

### 54. Give 4 differences between the central purposes of the IMF and the World Bank. Keep each difference to one clear point.

### 55. A customer needs help opening a basic bank account but has limited access to a branch. Explain the roles of KYC and a Business Correspondent in exactly 4 points, including one safeguard for the customer.

## Professional (56–60)

### 56. Explain how a central bank might use the repo rate, CRR, and open market operations to influence liquidity and inflation. Use exactly 5 numbered points: one for each tool, one for the main transmission path, and one for a limitation.

### 57. A trader pays ₹100 for goods weighing 1 kg, advertises them at “cost price,” and charges ₹100 while handing over only 800 g. Calculate the effective profit percentage on the goods actually delivered. Show exactly 4 steps: cost of the delivered quantity, revenue, profit, and profit percentage.

### 58. You are solving a multi-attribute floor puzzle and two possible arrangements remain after the first pass through the clues. Give exactly 6 steps for resolving the puzzle without guessing and checking whether the final solution is unique.

### 59. A customer asks which channel to use for a domestic payment: NEFT, RTGS, or UPI. Give a professional response in exactly 5 points, covering the information to clarify, the channel differences, how to handle changing limits or fees, a verification safeguard, and what to do for a time-critical transfer.

### 60. A rural enterprise needs finance to expand production, an exporter needs support for an overseas order, and a housing lender seeks sector-specific support. Explain which of NABARD, SIDBI, EXIM Bank, and NHB may be relevant in exactly 5 points: one point per institution and one point on how to avoid overstating their roles.
